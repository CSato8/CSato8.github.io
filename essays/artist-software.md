*Why is an Artist Learning Software Engineering*

To be quite honest, I had no concept of software engineering before starting my computer science classes. To me, software engineering was just another term people threw around to encapsulate anything to do with the software side of computers, but mainly coding. However, now that I am well within my years as a computer science student I would like to think I have grasped some idea of what software engineering is.

Software engineering is more than just coding, but rather the whole process of development from communicating and understanding the requirements of the software, to designing the structure, to testing if the software works as intended, to maintaining and improving code. All of these processes come together to boost the efficiency of the development process, create more reliable software, all while reducing cost. 

## Why am I Here?

While I had originally started my computer science journey for the sole purpose of pleasing my parents, I can't lie that the concepts of software engineering have definitely caught my attention. Although there are many different avenues that software engineering covers, I am mainly interested in the development of applications such as games or web apps. As an artist first, I am always looking for ways to incorporate my practice into everything I do. Naturally, as someone who has played copious amounts of video games when I was young, actually developing one was a pipe dream that I never thought would come true.

## What's in the Future?

As I continue on my journey through software engineering, I hope to improve my skills in coding and planning the structures of software. Even with all the classes I have taken so far, I can't say that coding is the most intuitive skill on the earth for me. Hopefully through ICS 314, a class I am currently taking, I am able to not only deepen my understanding of how coding works, but also improve the speed at which I write code. This also applies to planning and creating a structure for code. I often struggle with not knowing where to start, trying to take a large project all at once can be overwhelming.
